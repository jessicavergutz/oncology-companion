## When to use

Use this skill when processing new materials into the knowledge base, specifically during Phase 2 (Tagging) and Phase 3 (Insight Extraction).

## Core Principles

1. **Source Traceability**: Every insight must trace back to a specific file, line, and context.
2. **Layer Separation**: Distinguish between Philosophies (L1), Mental Models (L2), Heuristics (L3), Frameworks (L4), and Methodologies (L5).
3. **Atomic Extraction**: Extract insights in their most atomic form to allow for granular tagging and cross-referencing.

## Execution Pattern

1. **Scan Content**: Identify recurring themes and explicit "how-to" segments.
2. **Tag DNA**: Map the content to the 5 DNA layers.
3. **Verify Integrity**: Ensure no loss of context during the summarization/extraction process.
