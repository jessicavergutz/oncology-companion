# Project: Brazilian Bikini E-commerce in the US
**Executive Pitch & Action Plan - Summer 2026**

---

## 💎 Project Overview
Importing authentic Brazilian cut bikinis for sale via e-commerce in the United States, capitalizing on the market gap for body-enhancing swimwear while operating with an optimized logistics model and low initial capital requirement.

**Competitive Advantage:** The authentic "Brazilian Cut."

## 💰 1. Financial Viability (The Pilot Order)

The project presents an extremely low barrier to entry with premium-level profit margins.

* **Initial Parameters (30-unit batch):**
  * **Unit Cost:** $13 USD
  * **International Shipping:** $25 USD / kg
  * **Total Landed Cost:** ~$17.16 USD per unit.

* **Profit Projection:**
  * **Suggested Retail Price:** $55 USD
  * **Gross Profit per Unit:** $37.84 USD
  * **Gross Margin:** ~68%
  * **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) Target:** Up to $20 USD/sale to maintain profitability on the first order.

## ⚠️ 3. Risk Assessment & Critical Chokepoints

The project is highly viable, but there are operational bottlenecks that must be bulletproofed prior to launch:

1. **Sizing and Fit:** Brazilian bikinis run significantly smaller. A Brazilian "Medium" fits like an American "Small" or "X-Small." **Action:** Create an exact measurement conversion matrix (in inches) and highly visual sizing instructions on the product page.
2. **Return Rate:** High return rates will destroy the $37 profit margin.
 **Action:** Strict 14-day return policy requiring the hygiene liner seal to be 100% intact. Returns processed locally in the US.

---

## 🚀 4. Operational Roadmap (5-Week Plan)

* **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Foundation & Validation**
  * Secure the 30-piece pilot order.
  * Develop the sizing conversion matrix.
  * Set up domestic shipping infrastructure (e.g., PirateShip).

* **Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4): Digital Setup**
  * Build the Shopify store (Focus on high-quality imaging and fit demonstration).
  * Product photoshoot upon inventory arrival (On-body try-ons).
  * Produce comparative social media Reels: "US Cut vs. Brazilian Cut."

* **Phase 3 (Weeks 5+): Launch & Traction**
  * Organic launch via TikTok/Reels.
  * "Gifting" strategy targeting micro-influencers in FL, CA, and TX.
  * Initiate Meta Ads campaigns (Target: CAC < $20).

---

> **Conclave Final Verdict:** The "Bootstrapped" model via a small initial order validates the supply chain while mitigating the significant operational risk of sizing discrepancies. With strict discipline regarding inventory color selection and the absolute exclusion of unauthorized designer prints, the business holds high potential for scaling.
